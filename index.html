Privacy at a glance

PetCom is designed to help users organise pet profiles, care information, reminders, photographs, pet-passport information, maps, nearby pet services and other pet-care information.

PetCom stores pet records locally for offline use. Free access includes one local pet and does not provide a cloud account. Premium subscribers may choose to create a PetCom cloud account; after email verification and sync opt-in, account details, pet records and selected images are also stored through Google Firebase so they can appear on your other devices.

Some optional features communicate with third-party services when you choose to use them. For example, location and search information may be sent to Google Maps Platform and Google Places services when you request nearby pet services or map information.

PetCom does not sell your personal information.

PetCom does not use your personal information for cross-app advertising tracking.

PetCom does not require you to provide payment-card information directly to the app. Where purchases or subscriptions are offered, payments are processed by the relevant app store.

2. Information PetCom may process

Pet profile and care information

Information you choose to enter may include:

  • pet name;
  • species;
  • breed;
  • sex;
  • date of birth or age;
  • colour;
  • weight;
  • microchip information;
  • vaccination information;
  • treatment or care information;
  • medication or care notes;
  • reminders;
  • appointments;
  • pet-passport information; and
  • other pet-related records you choose to store.

Core pet records are stored locally on your device. If you choose a verified PetCom cloud account, the records are also synchronised to your private account in Google Cloud Firestore.

Owner, care-team and veterinary contact information

Information you choose to enter may also include an optional owner or profile name and email address; a care-team member's name, relationship, phone number and email address; and a chosen veterinary practice or veterinarian's name, phone number, email address and address.

This information is stored locally. It may also be synchronised to your private Google Cloud Firestore account if you enable verified PetCom cloud sync, or leave the device when you deliberately export, back up or share it with a destination you choose.

PetCom account information

If you create a cloud account, PetCom processes your first and last name, email address, password credential, email-verification status and a unique account identifier through Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; PetCom does not store it in the pet-record database.

Photos and documents

If you choose to add a pet photograph, use the camera, select an image, or import information from a pet passport or other document, PetCom may access your camera or photo library after you grant the relevant device permission.

Selected pet-passport or document images may contain information about the pet and may also contain personal information relating to an owner, keeper, veterinary professional or another person.

If you enable a verified cloud account, selected pet profile, passport and gallery images are uploaded to your private owner-scoped Firebase Storage area for sync across your devices. Images shared through your phone's share sheet are sent to the destination you choose.

You should only import documents that you are authorised to use.

Location information

If you enable location-based features, PetCom may process approximate or precise device location.

Location may be used for features including:

  • nearby veterinary practices;
  • nearby pet-related services;
  • maps;
  • weather-related information;
  • location-aware emergency information;
  • location-aware educational information; and
  • other features that depend on the user's current area.

Location access is optional unless a particular feature requires it.

Search information

When you search for places, services, addresses or other location-related information, PetCom may process the search terms and geographic search parameters needed to provide the requested results.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, PetCom may schedule notifications for reminders and other pet-care alerts. Notification permission is controlled through your device operating system.

Device and technical information

PetCom, the operating system and third-party SDKs or services integrated into PetCom may process limited technical information necessary to provide features, maintain security, diagnose errors and measure technical performance.

Depending on the SDK or service, this may include:

  • device model and operating-system version;

4. Local storage and optional cloud synchronisation

PetCom is designed so that core pet information is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

This may include pet profiles, care records, reminders and other information entered directly into the app.

You can use the app without a PetCom cloud account. If you choose to create or sign in to a verified account, PetCom uses Google Firebase as a service provider for authentication, synchronisation and recovery across devices:

  • Firebase Authentication processes the account email, password credential, verification status and account identifier.
  • Cloud Firestore stores account profile information and copies of pet profiles, care and health records, exercise history, passport details, care-team and veterinarian contacts, appointments, prescriptions and saved pet services.
  • Firebase Storage stores selected pet profile, passport and gallery images.

PetCom also records a pseudonymous device identifier, synchronisation timestamps, content hashes and deletion markers to keep devices consistent. Cloud copies are linked to your account and access is restricted to its signed-in owner by Firebase Security Rules. PetCom uses encrypted network connections for these services. Cloud Firestore and the photo bucket are configured in London, United Kingdom; Google may also process limited service data in other locations under its applicable terms.

Cloud sync starts after a verified sign-in and runs when the app starts, resumes, remains open periodically or when you choose to sync. Local data remains available offline. If you do not enable cloud sync, creating local pet records or images does not by itself upload those records to the PetCom cloud, although other online features may transmit the information described elsewhere in this policy.

5. Pet-passport scanning and Google ML Kit OCR

PetCom may use Google ML Kit text-recognition technology to assist with extracting text from a selected pet-passport or document image.

The image content and recognised text used for ML Kit text recognition are processed on the device. ML Kit does not send the input image, input text or resulting recognised content to Google servers for the purpose of text recognition.

The recognised information is presented for review or correction before it is used within the pet profile where the feature supports review.

Users should always verify automatically recognised information for accuracy.

Although recognition of the image and text occurs on-device, Google ML Kit may communicate limited technical, diagnostic and usage information to Google.

Depending on the ML Kit component and version, this can include information such as:

  • device and operating-system information;
  • app identifier and app version;
  • per-installation technical identifiers;
  • performance measurements;
  • API configuration information;
  • feature-use events; and
  • error information.

Google uses this technical information for purposes such as diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance, performance measurement, improvement of ML Kit and prevention of misuse.

PetCom does not intentionally send the content of your pet-passport image or the text recognised from it to Google through ML Kit's text-recognition processing.

PetCom may request location permission when you choose to use location-dependent functionality.

Where supported by your device, you may be able to choose between approximate and precise location.

Providing precise location may improve the accuracy of nearby-service results, but location access remains under your control through your device settings.

PetCom uses or may use Google Maps Platform, including Google Maps SDK and Google Places SDK / Places API (New), to provide maps, place information and nearby pet-service functionality.

When you request nearby places or use another Google Places-powered feature, information required to fulfil that request may be transmitted to Google.

Depending on the feature being used, this may include:

  • latitude and longitude;
  • a geographic search area;
  • search radius;
  • place or service categories;
  • search terms entered by you;
  • location bias or location restrictions;
  • language or region information;

Google Places may return information including business names, addresses, geographic coordinates, place categories, opening information, contact details, photographs, ratings or other place information depending on the information requested by PetCom.

Most Google Places requests may involve communication with Google's servers.

The Google Maps SDK may also automatically collect request and device metadata, internet-protocol (IP) address, a Maps SDK-specific pseudonymous identifier, stack traces and crash metrics, and map interaction events such as pan and zoom actions. Google describes this information as being used to operate, maintain and improve Google Maps Platform services. PetCom does not use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

PetCom uses this information to provide the location-based functionality that you requested.

PetCom does not use your location for cross-app advertising tracking.

Google processes information received through Google Maps Platform according to Google's applicable terms, policies and privacy practices.

PetCom displays Google or third-party attribution where required by the Google Maps Platform terms.

8. Weather and other network services

PetCom may use OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for address or place-name geocoding. Newer Android and iOS builds use Google Weather API as the primary forecast source, with MET Norway as a backup. Older Android builds may use Open-Meteo as the primary source, with MET Norway as a backup; some earlier iOS builds use MET Norway.

In builds using Google Weather, the app sends the selected location coordinates to a PetCom Firebase Cloud Function protected by Firebase App Check. The function forwards coordinates rounded to four decimal places to Google Weather using server-side credentials. Google receives the request from the PetCom server rather than directly from the phone. If Google Weather is unavailable, the phone requests a forecast directly from MET Norway. MET Norway may receive the phone's IP address, rounded coordinates and a PetCom user-agent with our support email.

For manual location searches and older weather builds, PetCom sends the information needed to fulfil the selected request. This may include rounded coordinates, a place name, postcode or another geographic reference. The app identifies the forecast source. Google Weather data carries Google Maps and weather-data attribution; MET Norway data carries source and CC BY 4.0 attribution.

Each provider processes requests under its applicable privacy terms. See Google, OpenStreetMap Foundation, Open-Meteo and MET Norway.
